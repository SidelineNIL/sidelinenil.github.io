Questions

Does it cost anything for athletes?

No. Signing up is free and athletes keep 100% of the deal amount. The 20% platform fee is paid by the business, on top of the deal.

When does the athlete get paid?

When both sides mark the deal complete, the payment is released from escrow to the athlete’s bank through Stripe. Bank transfers usually arrive within a few business days. Athletes set up payouts once, in the app.

What kinds of deals are there?

Social posts, in-store appearances, event promotions, podcasts and interviews, plus anything else a business wants to propose. Every deal is at least $100.

Which schools can use Sideline?

We’re launching at Penn State first, with businesses in and around State College. Athletes sign up with their school email.

What if a deal falls through?

Either side can end a deal in the app before it’s finished, with a reason. If that happens, the escrowed money goes back to the business.

Is a deal allowed under my school’s NIL rules?

NIL rules differ by school, conference, and state, and they change. Athletes are responsible for following the rules that apply to them, including any disclosure their school requires. Sideline doesn’t give compliance or legal advice. Check with your athletic department if you’re unsure.
